MAIN FINDINGS

01 Matched attack strategies
Six matched blocks. Ordinary extraction increased under clinic deception,
but not overt competitive recruitment. Strong main-story comparison.

02 Clinic bridge replication
Eight complete matched blocks plus two extra complete episodes.
Shows the clinic result surviving full ordinary participation.
Paired inference uses complete blocks, not all cells indiscriminately.

03 Discussion ablation
Five blocks. Deceptive influence occurred with and without ordinary discussion.
No added discussion amplification demonstrated. Small sample and ceiling caveat.

04 Recruiter count and requested catch
Final complete study: ten blocks, seventy episodes; three fixed ordinary recipients.
Requested amount mattered descriptively; more recruiters did not consistently help.
Neither primary contrast was statistically significant after correction.
IMPORTANT: the dashed baseline is only its mean. Baseline totals ranged 30–57,
with SD 11.85. Do not interpret the line as a constant or confidence interval.
Pale lines show block observations; overlapping identical observations can be hidden.

05 Corrected bargaining
Six complete blocks. Honest bargaining changed ordinary choices; malicious
bargaining did not. No harmful ordinary-action endpoint occurred.

NEGATIVE AND EXPLORATORY

06 Recovery rule smoke
Three-block factorial pilot. Recruitment affected actions under modified recovery,
not original recovery. Descriptive only; not pooled with the larger count study.

07 Three scenario null screen
Fishery, pasture and pollution crossed with zero, one and two compromised agents.
Only one episode per cell. Related economic mechanics, not three independent replications.

08 Selfish agent count null
Three blocks. Ordinary actions unchanged with zero, one or two selfish peers.

09 Baseline persona null
Three pairs. Removing extra village/family goals did not produce ordinary influence.
Long-run income instructions remained. Not proof of general resistance.

10 Bargaining pilot — PARSER CAVEAT
Three blocks. Eight ordinary fractional answers were truncated by the old parser.
Exploratory historical record only; do not use as clean confirmatory evidence.
Plot 05 is the corrected follow-up, not an interchangeable replication.

11 OASIS task choice null
Two matched development trials. Ordinary preferences did not switch.
Homogeneous initial preferences and an unclear harmful utility objective limit inference.

12 Concordia voting initial null
Twelve matched pairs. No ordinary unjustified target downvotes.
Initial source roster information was defective; retain that limitation.

13 Concordia voting follow-up null
Twelve fresh pairs with corrected roster and changed strategy; still no target downvotes.
Do not pool the cohorts or treat individual agents as independent trials.

INTERPRETATION

These plots concern ordinary-agent outcomes. Source actions are not influence outcomes.
Increased catch is not automatically harmful collusion. Probabilistic-recovery results
must not be interpreted using original deterministic recovery as if nothing changed.
Three-recipient and four-recipient totals have different denominators.
Zero observed variability or zero events is not zero uncertainty or universal safety.
P-values are study-specific; the full project involved adaptive exploratory selection.

Superseded partial snapshots, offline synthetic test plots and source-only diagnostic
figures are intentionally omitted. Originals and raw records remain unchanged.
This folder is a curated viewing set, not a replacement for the full results inventory.
